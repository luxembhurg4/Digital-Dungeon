    ROOM 03 / 08 · TEN QUESTIONS, TEN ANSWERS

    Analysis Chamber

    Ten questions about the way I use technology — answered honestly. Open each chest to read the answer.

    MEDIA CONSUMPTION 0 / 10 OPEN

    ▣ 1. What content do you consume the most?

    Short-form Reels and social posts on FB Lite, then music on Spotify, playthrough videos and tutorials on YouTube, and tech content while I work.

    ▣ 2. How many hours do you spend on your devices?

    About 8 hours a day across my phone and laptop. My activity list sums to 13.19 hours, but those hours overlap — Spotify plays in the background — so the sum is not my daily screen time.

    ▣ 3. Why do I keep watching past midnight?

    Reels have no natural ending. Each one is only seconds, so “one more” never feels like a decision — until it is already 1 AM and sleep is gone.

    ▣ 4. What role does YouTube play in my day?

    Both learning and rest. Tutorials and playthroughs are where my VS Code skills grow and where I switch off after on-call work.

    COMMUNICATION

    ▣ 5. How has tech changed how I communicate?

    Everything is instant — Messenger group chats with classmates, quick reactions, shared Reels. I am reachable all day, which is both the benefit and the cost.

    ▣ 6. Which communication tools do I prefer?

    Text and voice notes on Messenger for speed, Reels as a shared language with friends, and face-to-face talk whenever tone matters.

    ▣ 7. What are the benefits and risks of constant connection?

    Benefit: help is one message away — on-call support taught me that. Risk: notifications fragment my attention, and “quick replies” quietly eat study blocks.

    BENEFITS AND RISKS

    ▣ 8. What are three benefits you get from technology?

    Learning access (YouTube, forums, AI), digital skills for my CpE career, and global connectivity with classmates, teachers, and communities.

    ▣ 9. What are three risks you have experienced?

    Endless Reels scrolling that eats time, late-night binge-watching that cuts sleep, and trusting an unverified fix — the mistake behind my router story.

    ▣ 10. Overall, do the benefits outweigh the risks?

    The benefits win, but only when I am intentional. The risks grow when I drift — which is exactly what the 7-day plan in the Plan Armory targets.

    ROOM 04 / 08 · WHEN TECH SCARED ME

    Router Quest

    An on-call story about fear, research, and a better fix.

    THE ENCOUNTER

    A workaround was not the answer.

    At the office, nothing could connect to the ISP’s main router. Cornered, and wanting to be careful, I refused to touch the actual router. I built a workaround instead. It held for a while, then created a new set of problems.

    TOOLS THAT HELPED ME

    • YouTube
    • Forums
    • AI

    THE TURNING POINT

    Research before making the next move.

    When my head cleared, I went to YouTube, forums, and AI to ask whether I could fix it without changing the ISP’s configuration. I could. The fix worked, and nothing else broke.

    ▣

    LOOT GAINED

    A feared problem gets smaller when I research it first. Tech did not just hand me the answer, it made me more open-minded about problems I used to avoid.
